Unmapped demo track

A 12 km bike ride through central Berlin, to try Unmapped without travelling.

Download GPX file

If Safari shows text instead of downloading: use the ZIP version, then tap the ZIP in the Files app to unpack it.

  1. Tap "Download GPX file". Safari saves it to Files → Downloads.
  2. Open Unmapped → tab "Reisen" → import button at the bottom left ("Reise importieren") → "GPX-Datei" → Downloads → unmapped-demo-berlin.gpx.
  3. The ride appears as a journey; tabs "Welt" and "Fortschritt" now show Berlin uncovered along the route.
